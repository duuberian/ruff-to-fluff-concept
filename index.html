BEFORE THE FIRST VISIT

Less guesswork.
More tail wags.

What should I tell the groomer?

Your dog’s breed, coat type and previous grooming experience are useful starting points. Mention any sensitivities or worries so you can discuss the right approach together.

What does a groom cost?

This concept deliberately doesn’t invent a price list. The groomer would confirm the service, suitability, price and time with you directly.

Is this an instant booking?

No. The form below is a working demonstration only. A live request system could let Melanie review each enquiry before confirming anything.

Where is the parlour?
